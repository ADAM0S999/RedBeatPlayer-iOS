# RedBeat Player for iOS

> Swift & SwiftUI offline music player designed with a **Red & Black Theme**.  
> Created & Designed by **[@LM7BOS](https://instagram.com/LM7BOS)**.

---

## 📱 Features

- **Red & Black Theme:** Deep `#0A0A0A` background with `#E53935` crimson accents.
- **AVAudioEngine & AVAudioUnitEQ:** Parametric 5-band Equalizer (60Hz, 230Hz, 910Hz, 3.6kHz, 14kHz), Bass Boost, and presets.
- **Lock Screen & Control Center:** Complete `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` and `MPRemoteCommandCenter` integration.
- **Local Library & Playlists:** Offline playback from app Documents storage with sorting and search.
- **Direct Audio Downloader:** Download songs and podcasts directly from URLs into your local library.
- **Wi-Fi File Transfer:** Browser-based drag-and-drop file transfer over local Wi-Fi.
- **Developer Credit:** Direct link and social card for [@LM7BOS](https://instagram.com/LM7BOS).

---

## 🛠️ Requirements

- iOS 16.0+
- Xcode 15+ / Swift 5.9+
