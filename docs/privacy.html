Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Short version: RedBeat Player does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All music files stay on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

1. Who We Are

RedBeat Player ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal offline music player developed by @LM7BOS. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information when you use the RedBeat Player iOS application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal information whatsoever. The table below summarizes what the app accesses and why:

Data / Permission Purpose Shared?
Audio files in your Documents folder Playback only — files you explicitly download or import Never
Files app access Import music files you choose Never
Network access (optional) Download audio from URLs you manually enter Never
Now Playing info (lock screen) Display current song title/artist on lock screen Never
Crash reports Not collected Never
Analytics / usage data Not collected Never
Device identifiers (IDFA, etc.) Not collected Never

3. Music Downloads

When you use the in-app URL downloader, the app downloads audio directly from the URL you enter to your iPhone's local storage. These files are saved in the app's private Documents directory and are never transmitted to any third-party server. You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to download and use any audio content.

4. Local Storage Only

All app data — including your music library, playlist configurations, and equalizer settings — is stored exclusively on your device. If you delete the app, all app data is permanently removed from your device.

5. No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

RedBeat Player contains:

  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics frameworks (Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No crash reporting services (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • No social media SDKs
  • No third-party tracking of any kind

6. Children's Privacy

RedBeat Player is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or any other age).

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at this same URL. We will also note the change in the app update release notes.
